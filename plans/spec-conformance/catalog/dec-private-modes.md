# DEC Private Modes Catalog

One row per numeric DEC private mode (DECSET/DECRST pair). Every variant of `NamedPrivateMode` in `crates/vte/src/ansi/types.rs` has a row. Set/reset are one row with a dual-form `Sequence` column — `catalog_coverage_check` expands each row to `(CSI, [?], Ps, h)` and `(CSI, [?], Ps, l)` tuples at check time.

Ownership: Section 01 (bootstrap) creates the rows; Section 09 (DEC Private Modes full verification) drives them to `verified`.

Non-DECSET/DECRST question-mark sequences (DECST8C `CSI ? 5 W`, DECRQM `CSI ? Ps $ p`, XTSAVE/XTRESTORE `CSI ? Ps s`/`CSI ? Ps r`, `CSI ? u` keyboard query) live in `ecma-48.md` and `xterm-ctlseqs.md`.
