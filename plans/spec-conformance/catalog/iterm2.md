# iTerm2 Proprietary Sequences Catalog

iTerm2 introduced several proprietary sequences via OSC 1337 and other numeric OSCs. Section 10 (OSC Suite) owns the non-image OSC 1337 sub-ops (`SetMark`, `RemoteHost`, `CurrentDir`, `Copy`, `ReportCellSize`, `SetUserVar`, `ShellIntegrationVersion`) and their verification; Section 14 (iTerm2 Inline Images) owns `File=` and the image-transmit chain.
